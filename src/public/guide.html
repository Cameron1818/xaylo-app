Your getting-started guide

A small setup.
A clearer picture.

Start collecting your own journey data and connect the accounts you want to include in your reports.

Open your workspace ↗

Create your Xaylo workspace.

Sign in with your email link, or open Xaylo from an installed HighLevel location. Add your services, target audience, goals and constraints under Connections.

Add your website and tracking script.

Enter your website’s exact origin in Settings, then copy your script from Tracking into the site header. For a GHL website, use the site’s custom head tracking area.

For example, an origin is https://yourbusiness.com. Tracking starts after installation; it does not backfill earlier website visits.

Connect your advertising and optional CRM.

In Connections, authorise Google Ads and select your advertising account. Connect your own HighLevel location if you want customer and opportunity summaries.

GHL is optional for a website + Ads setup

Define a confirmed conversion.

Connect window.Xaylo.conversion() to a successful booking, purchase or enquiry. Use consistent campaign UTMs so your sources are recognisable.

A developer or website automation may be needed for this step. Linking the visit to a specific GHL contact also requires a signed contact link; it is not inferred from the visitor’s browser.

Explore, report and personalise.

Follow visitor paths in Overview. Open Reports to read source analysis and generate recommendations. In Journeys, create rules and mark page elements you want to personalise.

Google Ads conversions, website conversions and CRM pipeline outcomes are shown with their own definitions. Recommendations are advisory; you decide which changes to make.

A few useful answers.

Before you start
Do I need a HighLevel account?

No. You can create a Xaylo account, install the tracking script on your website and connect Google Ads. HighLevel is an optional connection for business information, contact segments and opportunity outcomes.

Can I use Xaylo on a GHL website?

Yes. Add the tracking script to your GHL website’s custom head tracking area. Connect your own GHL location to include CRM context. The script also works on a standard HTML website with a separately connected GHL CRM.

What does the AI use to make recommendations?

Xaylo-managed prompts combine your business goals and audience with application-calculated traffic, advertising and CRM summary metrics. Reports include supporting evidence, confidence and source limitations. You can also access reporting tools through an authorised MCP connection.

Will Xaylo change my adverts or marketing automatically?

Advertising reports and AI recommendations are advisory. Xaylo can personalise website elements you explicitly mark and can add configured GHL contact tags after a linked conversion. You control published journeys and any changes made to your adverts or workflows.

Will my contacts and conversions be linked automatically?

Connecting GHL makes contact and opportunity summaries available. Website conversions need an explicit confirmed-conversion event. Linking a particular visit to a known GHL contact requires a signed contact link from a trusted integration. Xaylo does not assume that an anonymous visitor is a specific CRM contact.

Does Xaylo include contract documents or payment revenue?

The current GHL integration reads contacts and opportunities. It does not read signed contract documents or collected payments. CRM opportunity values are pipeline values, and Google Ads conversion value follows the conversion actions configured in that account.

What happens when my free allowance ends?

You need a subscription for further AI reports and, after a time-limited trial expires, new source analysis. Your saved reports remain readable. Paid reporting periods extend up to 90 days; your current allowance and billing options are available in the dashboard.
